01 Lifecycle segmentation

Turning product usage into lifecycle strategy

Decision
Use lifecycle behavior and product-use preferences to guide outreach and retention planning.
My Role
Analyzed individual-plan usage with SQL and Mode and developed lifecycle segments for Marketing and account teams.
Evidence
78K+ individual-plan users in Q2 2025; engagement, retention, churn, and relative use of Stock Media, the Music Library, and Maker.
Outcome
Lifecycle campaign open rates improved by approximately 10% relative to the previous quarter. Segmentation informed three enterprise renewal plans.

Approach — make behavior actionable

I analyzed 78K+ individual-plan users in Q2 2025 using SQL and Mode. I examined engagement, retention, and churn alongside relative use of Stock Media, the Music Library, and Maker, including preferences within music and stock-media categories.

I translated observed product use into lifecycle segments that Marketing could use for more relevant outreach and account teams could use to inform retention planning.

02 AI Toolkit

Finding the role of authenticity in AI-powered creative workflows

Decision
Identify the AI-related customer needs and use cases for Product and Creative to consider prioritizing.
My Role
Synthesized research and product-use evidence into an AI product-use playbook and priority use cases for Product and Creative.
Evidence
Enterprise interview themes on authenticity; email follow-ups and pop-up surveys; competitor AI research, industry context, and product-use evidence.
Outcome
Produced an AI product-use playbook and presented priority use cases to Product and Creative.

Approach — use customer values to frame AI opportunities

I reviewed direct competitors’ AI approaches and broader industry trends, including concerns about authenticity in AI-generated stock media. I gathered customer feedback through email follow-ups and pop-up surveys, and conducted in-depth interviews with enterprise users about why they valued and continued using Storyblocks.

Authenticity emerged primarily from enterprise interview themes. The industry discussion and concerns about competitors’ AI-generated stock media provided context for interpreting those themes.

I synthesized the research and product-use evidence into an AI product-use playbook and priority use cases, which I presented to Product and Creative.

03 Competitive intelligence

Turning competitive analysis into sharper positioning

Decision
Identify meaningful differences between Storyblocks and alternative creative platforms to support positioning discussions.
My Role
Kept established competitor research current and researched emerging AI products with similar capabilities and positioning.
Evidence
Existing competitor profiles, updated product and feature information, and research on emerging AI products.
Outcome
Maintained a current competitive view for Marketing and Sales positioning discussions.

Approach — connect product differences to customer value

I researched competitor offerings to distinguish feature differences from their implications for enterprise creative teams. I organized the comparison around content, workflow tools, licensing, and subscription structure, connecting individual capabilities to the value of the overall offer.

Leading competitors had already been researched when I joined. I kept their feature and product-positioning profiles up to date and continued researching emerging AI products with similar capabilities or positioning.
